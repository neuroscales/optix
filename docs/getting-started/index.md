# Getting started

Two pages take you from nothing to a first corrected image.

- [Installation](installation.md) lists the requirements and installs optix
  from PyPI or from source.
- [Quick tour](quick-tour.md) simulates a stack of camera frames with a known
  flat field, fits an estimator to it and applies the result, in about five
  minutes.

The [Concepts](../concepts/index.md) pages then explain the ideas that every
algorithm relies on.
